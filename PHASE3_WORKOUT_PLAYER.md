# Phase 3 — Workout Player

UI-only refinement of the workout execution surface.

## Goals
- Make the exercise/action hierarchy immediate.
- Give reps/timer the strongest visual focus.
- Keep target and previous-performance context available but subordinate.
- Make RIR/fatigue collection compact and consistent.
- Use Black + Lime consistently for active/progress states.
- Improve mobile touch targets and safe-area behavior.
- Keep recovery and EMOM states visually distinct without changing behavior.

## Constraints
No business logic, calculations, persisted data, workout behavior, state transitions, or functionality were changed.
