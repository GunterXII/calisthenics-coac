# V17.3 — Adaptive Coach Core Hardening

## Scope
- Program-facing goal state uses repeatable performance once enough comparable evidence exists; `best` remains an achievement metric.
- Weekly stimulus is explicitly separated from workload/fatigue.
- RIR and logged quality are used as conservative modifiers of productive hypertrophy stimulus.
- Hypertrophy is evaluated per muscle, not as one global weekly floor.
- The Coach can recommend protecting the priority, holding it, adding hypertrophy, or reducing secondary work when fatigue is high.
- OAP / FLPU / Front Lever Touch remain skill goals; the classic Front Lever is a supporting capacity.

## Important product constraint
The numerical stimulus and productive-set values are **planning heuristics**. They are not physiological measurements and should never be shown to the athlete as exact science.

## UX rule
RIR and fatigue remain simple athlete inputs. The athlete does not need to understand the internal stimulus model.
