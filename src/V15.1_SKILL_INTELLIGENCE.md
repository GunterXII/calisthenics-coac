# V15.1 — Skill Intelligence Engine

## Purpose
Turn workout logs into conservative coach recommendations without auto-promoting the athlete.

## Decision hierarchy
1. RED / REGRESS when recent joint-pain signal is >= 4/5.
2. YELLOW / REVIEW when pain is 3/5, performance drops >=20% without a readiness explanation, or static-attempt quality falls below 67% clean.
3. GREEN / PROGRESS only after the progression standard is met in 2 consecutive qualifying exposures.
4. YELLOW / HOLD after one qualifying exposure.
5. BUILDING when data is insufficient.

## Inputs
- Exercise-specific prescription and catalog metadata.
- Consecutive workout exposures.
- Reps / holds / EMOM results.
- RIR when recorded.
- Static hold quality when recorded by the workout player.
- Readiness and wrist/elbow pain associated with the session.

## Exercise-specific rules
- Front Touch: 3+ free attempts, >=8 s each, all attempts clean.
- Assisted Front Touch: 3+ attempts, >=8 s each, RIR >=1 when supplied.
- OAP: 6+ attempts, >=2 successful >=2-rep exposures per arm, RIR >=1 when supplied.
- Assisted OAP: 6 reps at >=5 each, RIR >=1 when supplied.
- Full FL Pull-up: 5 reps x >=4 with clean quality.
- Band FL Pull-up: 3 reps x >=6, RIR >=1 when supplied.
- EMOM standards: minimum prescribed per-minute output with <15% drop-off.
- Generic static/reps skills fall back to catalog range + clean quality + RIR when available.

## Research rationale
The engine uses autoregulation principles: performance is interpreted together with readiness and fatigue instead of treating a fixed target as sufficient evidence. RIR is used as a practical internal-effort signal, while consecutive exposures reduce the chance that one unusually good session triggers an inappropriate progression.
