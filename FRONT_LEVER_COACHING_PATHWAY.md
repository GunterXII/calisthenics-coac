# Athlete-specific Front Lever Coaching Pathway

Baseline used by Coach:
- Front Touch best: 5 s
- Front Lever Pull-up best: 4 reps
- OAP best: 2 consecutive reps

Programming rule: do not prescribe a hold range above the athlete's current demonstrated capacity. Free Front Touch currently uses 2–4 s quality holds with 3–4 min rest. Assisted Front Touch is used for additional volume at 6–10 s using the lightest band that preserves the same pattern.

Promotion path:
1. Consolidate clean Front Touch to 8 s.
2. Introduce Wide Front Lever Touch progressively, first with controlled/assisted exposure, then longer clean free holds.
3. Build strong wide-touch capacity before introducing Straight Arm Touch (SAT). SAT is treated as the long-term end goal, not a near-term prescription.

This ordering is consistent with calisthenics coaching sources that place Front Lever Touch after a strong full Front Lever and describe advanced progressions through harder touch variations; a current specialist progression resource includes Front Lever Touch, Wide Front Lever Pull-up/Touch work and Straight Arm Touch as separate advanced skills.

## Programming rule
- Do not program dedicated Full Front Lever sets. The 15-second full FL is already consolidated.
- Spend recovery capacity on Front Touch, FL Pull-up, and OAP progression.
