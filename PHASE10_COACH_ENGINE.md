# Phase 10 — Coach Engine 2.0

- Exercise-level contextual decisions: PROGRESS / HOLD / REDUCE_VOLUME / REGRESS / REVIEW.
- Same-prescription comparison now includes the selected band.
- Weekly workload/recovery and readiness influence the decision.
- Low-output sessions with low RIR are treated as potential fatigue before regression.
- Primary skill work is protected from automatic volume reduction; lower-priority volume should be cut first.
- Post-session Coach Verdict now exposes per-exercise decisions and concise reasons.
