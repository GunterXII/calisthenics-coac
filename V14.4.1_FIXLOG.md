# V14.4.1 — Front Touch / Catalog Coach Fixes

## Workout fixes
- Fixed StaticSkill attempt lock: `stopLock` is reset before every new attempt, including after rest/skip-rest.
- Front Touch is now **3 free quality attempts**, not 7.
- Assisted Front Touch is **3 x 5–8 sec** after the free work.
- Assisted Front Touch defaults to the **Purple 25–40 lb** heavier loop.
- Assisted Front Touch keeps Blue 15–25 lb as the lighter progression.
- Front Touch remains **8–12 sec** with a 12 sec cap.
- Existing unilateral consecutive-exercise reset remains protected by `key={block.id}` on `BlockPlayer`.

## Catalog / coach layer
- Supabase exercise catalog is already wired through `fetchExerciseCatalog()`.
- Edit Plan stores a stable `catalogExerciseId` and filters compatible replacements by skill/movement pattern.
- Supabase currently contains the exercise catalog + progression graph used by the coach layer.
