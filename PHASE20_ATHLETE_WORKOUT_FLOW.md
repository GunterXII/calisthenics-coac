# V19.2 — Athlete Workout Flow

## Goal
Make the daily workout feel like a guided execution flow rather than a form-heavy tracker.

## Flow
1. 10-second readiness check.
2. Warm-up.
3. One exercise at a time, with current phase + exercise purpose visible.
4. Log the minimum useful signal: reps/hold, then RIR; session fatigue is the simpler global signal.
5. Automatic rest with skip control.
6. Session completion.
7. Coach review is the first recommended post-workout action.
8. Optional post-workout mobility.
9. Done.

## UX rules
- Technical terms are explained at the point of entry.
- Avoid asking for duplicate fatigue information.
- Do not silently change the plan.
- Show the athlete why the next exercise exists.
- Prefer one clear primary action per state.
